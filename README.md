# Angel Wars: Heaven's Armies

Free, mobile-first Christian-fantasy action strategy game from Light & Life Games. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Controls
- WASD / arrows: move
- Space or Attack: sword strike
- Q or Recruit: spend 20 Light to summon a guardian
- E or Rally: toggle army follow/defend
- F or Wall: spend 25 Light to build a defense
- R or Skill: use the selected class ability
- On mobile, use the directional and action buttons.

## Current scope
Ten distinct levels, three playable classes with separate combat engines, five enemy families, two multi-phase boss fights, guardian formations, placeable walls, class abilities, sound, responsive controls, and locally saved progress. No external libraries or accounts.

## Class combat
- **Vanguard:** timed three-hit sword combo, wide finishing cleave, Radiant Charge, and inspired guardian damage.
- **Warden:** shield-bash impact timing, heavy knockback and stun, stronger walls, and a healing Sanctuary Dome.
- **Seraph:** auto-aimed staff projectiles, ranged casting movement, ally healing, and staggered Sevenfold Starfall strikes.

The Ash Captain now telegraphs arena slams and enters a faster reinforcement phase. The Prince of Night fires radial void bolts, opens damaging rifts, and becomes more aggressive in phase two.

## Biblical framing
Original fantasy inspired by Revelation 12:7; the game does not imply that its fictional elements are directly recorded in Scripture.
