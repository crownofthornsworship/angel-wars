# Angel Wars graphics and gameplay roadmap

## Completed in the class-combat repair

- Three visibly different class previews: Vanguard, Warden, and Seraph.
- Three complete class-specific attack frames with the weapon integrated into the character pose.
- Removed the separate floating-weapon renderer that caused duplicate weapons.
- Vanguard uses a broad sword sweep, Warden uses a shield bash with knockback and brief stun, and Seraph uses a ranged staff bolt.
- Preserved keyboard, touch controls, ten-level campaign, saves, bosses, army commands, and fortress systems.

## Completed in the deep class and boss update

- Vanguard now has a three-hit melee combo, oversized finishing cleave, guardian inspiration, and a target-seeking Radiant Charge.
- Warden now uses a timed shield impact with knockback, stun, recovery, fortified construction, and a healing Sanctuary Dome.
- Seraph now aims staff bolts toward enemies, visibly casts while hovering, heals allies, and calls seven staggered star strikes.
- Attack damage now lands at each class's visible impact frame instead of immediately on button press.
- The Ash Captain telegraphs charging arena slams and changes speed while summoning phase-two reinforcements.
- The Prince of Night uses radial void bolts, telegraphed rifts, and a more aggressive second phase.
- Mobile movement releases safely on pointer loss, controls respect phone safe areas, and portrait/landscape HUD overlap is reduced.

## Phase 2 — full character animation sets

- Give every class consistent idle, movement, attack anticipation, attack impact, recovery, hurt, skill, and victory frames.
- Anchor every frame at the feet so characters do not jump or resize between poses.
- Add short animation timing windows so damage lands when the weapon visually connects.
- Give recruited guardians their own unified armor and attack cycle instead of borrowing the Vanguard artwork.

## Phase 3 — readable battle animation

- Give each enemy family a distinct silhouette, movement animation, attack, hurt reaction, and defeat effect.
- Add clear class-colored hit flashes, impact sparks, knockback, and subtle screen shake for bosses.
- Replace generic particle bursts with sword trails, shield shockwaves, staff projectiles, healing light, and fortress impacts.
- Keep effects behind controls and HUD so mobile play remains readable.

## Phase 4 — battlefield and fortress polish

- Create several level-specific battlefield compositions rather than relying on one repeated background.
- Add foreground depth, animated clouds, banners, distant armies, weather, and level-specific hazards.
- Build visible fortress damage states and construction stages for walls and upgrades.
- Give both bosses unique arenas, entrances, attacks, phase changes, and defeat sequences.

## Phase 5 — deeper class gameplay

- Vanguard: melee combos, charged cleave, guardian-leading aura.
- Warden: directional block, shield charge, deployable protective wall.
- Seraph: aimed staff bolts, area healing, targeted starfall.
- Add class-specific upgrades between levels so replaying the campaign feels meaningfully different.

## Release gate

- Verify each class is recognizable at phone size before reading its label.
- Verify no attack frame ever shows two weapons.
- Verify attack damage matches the visible contact moment.
- Test portrait phone, landscape phone, tablet, and desktop with no hidden controls or HUD overlap.
- Maintain smooth play on mid-range mobile hardware and preserve save compatibility.
